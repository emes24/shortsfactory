Shorts Factory

A personal tool for preparing and uploading short educational videos.

Shorts Factory is a private, single-user tool. It assembles short vertical videos about personal finance and economics from text scripts, stock footage and a voice track, then uploads the finished file to its owner's own TikTok account as a draft for review before publishing.

The tool is not offered as a service to other people, has no user accounts, and is operated by one person on one computer.
